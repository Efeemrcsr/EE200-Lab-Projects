# EE200 Laboratory Projects & Circuit Designs

## Overview
This repository contains a comprehensive collection of laboratory experiments, circuit simulations, and technical reports completed for the EE200 course. It serves as a portfolio of practical analog circuit design and hardware verification.

## Technical Skills Demonstrated
* **Circuit Simulation & Analysis:** Extensive use of LTspice for transient, AC sweep, and DC operating point analyses.
* **Component-Level Design:** Building and testing analog circuit topographies using operational amplifiers, BJT and MOSFET transistors, Zener diodes, and passive RLC networks.
* **Hardware Assembly & Debugging:** Physical prototyping, soldering, and troubleshooting of electronic equipment to bridge the gap between theoretical models and real-world implementation.

## Repository Structure
* `/Lab-6`: Function Generator
* `/Lab-7`: Bipolar Junction Transistor (BJT)
* `/Lab-8`: AM Radio Reciever
* `/Lab-9`: Multistage BJT Amplifier
* `/Lab-10`: Common Source Amplifier
